# **GST1 \- Global Stocktake Explorer - Methodology **

The first Global Stocktake concluded on 13 December 2023 at the UN climate summit COP28 in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. The Global Stocktake Explorer allows searching the full text of all the inputs to the first Global Stocktake at once. Giving you access to search through over 1,800 documents and 200,000 pages of inputs to the first Global Stocktake. Documents in all languages are auto-translated to English. 

**Scope of documents**

The Global Stocktake Explorer has over 1,800 inputs to the Global Stocktake defined according to decision 19/CMA.1 para 37\. The documents were last downloaded from the UNFCCC portals on the 24th of May 2023, after the March 2023 cut-off date for inputs to the Global Stocktake. These inputs have been sourced from the following portals:

* [Global Stocktake Information Portal](https://unfccc.int/topics/global-stocktake/information-portal?field_document_topic_target_id=All&field_document_type_target_id=4174&field_calculated_author_type_target_id=All&field_document_ca_target_id=)  
* [NDC Registry](https://unfccc.int/NDCREG)  
* [Adaptation Communications Registry](https://unfccc.int/ACR)  
* [NAP Central Portal](https://napcentral.org/submitted-naps)  
* [TNA Portal](https://unfccc.int/ttclear/tna/reports.html)  
* [Fast-Start Finance Country Reports](https://unfccc.int/climatefinance?submissions)  
* [IPCC Website](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/)

The Global Stocktake Explorer contains the following input documents:

* Party submissions  
  * Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC)  
  * National Adaptation Plan (NAP)  
  * Long-term Low-emission Development Strategy (LT-LEDS)  
  * Biennial Update Report (BUR)  
  * Biennial Report (BR)  
  * National Communication (NC)  
  * National Inventory Report (NIR)  
  * Adaptation Communication (AC)  
* Non-party  
  * Facilitative Sharing of Views Report (FSVR)  
  * Fast-Start Finance Report  
  * Global Stocktake Synthesis Report  
  * Intersessional Document  
  * IPCC Report  
  * Pre-Session Document  
  * Progress Reports  
  * Publication  
  * Report

  * Summary Report
